About ECET

Engineering Common Entrance Test (ECET)

ECET Portal is an integrated online learning platform designed to support diploma students in their ECET preparation journey. It offers comprehensive study materials, semester-wise notes, previous examination papers, real-time mock tests, detailed result analysis, and an intuitive interface that enhances the learning experience. The platform aims to provide students with a reliable, efficient, and interactive environment to strengthen their knowledge, evaluate their performance, and improve their chances of success in the Engineering Common Entrance Test.
